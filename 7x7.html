Functional Foods for Diabetes

  • Millets (Kambu, Thinai, Samai, Varagu, Kuthiraivali, Ragi) – Low GI, high fiber, prevents sugar spikes.
  • Karuppatti (Palm Jaggery) – Natural sweetener, rich in iron and minerals.
  • Kollu (Horse Gram) – Protein-rich, improves insulin sensitivity.
  • Murungai Keerai (Drumstick Leaves) – Regulates blood sugar, rich in antioxidants.
  • Vendhayam (Fenugreek Seeds) – Soluble fiber, slows carbohydrate absorption.
  • Bitter Gourd (Pavakkai) – Natural blood sugar regulator.
  • Navadhanya (Nine-Grain Mix) – Provides balanced nutrition for sustained energy.
  • Turmeric & Amla – Anti-inflammatory, supports pancreas function.
  • Ginger & Cinnamon – Helps glucose metabolism, reduces insulin resistance.
  • Ash Gourd & Guava – Low GI fruits, rich in vitamins.

7×7 Days Meal Plan (With Functional Foods)

Functional Foods Breakdown for Each Meal

Breakfast Functional Foods:
  • Kambu, Thinai, Samai, Varagu, Ragi (Millets) – Low GI, fiber-rich.
  • Karuppatti (Palm Jaggery) – Natural sweetener with minerals.
  • Murungai Keerai Chutney – Regulates blood sugar levels.
Mid-Morning Functional Snacks:
  • Fenugreek Water – Lowers post-meal sugar spikes.
  • Amla Juice – Boosts insulin sensitivity.
  • Walnuts, Almonds, Peanuts – Healthy fats for glucose control.
  • Bitter Gourd Juice – Purifies blood and balances insulin.
Lunch Functional Foods:
  • Brown Rice / Millets – Sustained energy release, high fiber.
  • Kollu Sambar / Vendhaya Kuzhambu – Balances digestion, prevents sugar spikes.
  • Murungai Keerai / Keerai Masiyal – Strengthens immune function.
  • Puli Kuzhambu (Tamarind Curry) – Aids digestion, controls sugar levels.
Evening Snacks Functional Foods:
  • Sprouted Kollu, Roasted Chana, Boiled Green Gram – Protein-rich, slow sugar absorption.
  • Herbal Teas (Tulsi, Lemon, Ginger, Cinnamon) – Regulates metabolism.
  • Ash Gourd Juice – Cools body, supports digestion.
Dinner Functional Foods:
  • Ragi Dosa, Varagu Idli, Broken Wheat Upma – Low GI alternatives to rice.
  • Bitter Gourd Chutney / Herbal Rasam – Detoxifies body, regulates sugar.
  • Murungai Soup / Kollu Adai – Strengthens the pancreas.

Key Benefits of This Meal Plan

  • Affordable & Easily Available: Uses Tamil Nadu’s staple ingredients.
  • Nutritionally Balanced: Includes fiber, proteins, and essential minerals.
  • Blood Sugar Friendly: All meals have a low glycemic impact.
  • Traditional & Healthy: Based on Siddha & Ayurvedic food wisdom.
  • Easy to Follow & Prepare: Simple home-cooked meals for middle-class families.

Takeaway for Y-Diet Subscribers

  • Follow this 7×7 Days Meal Plan consistently for 4 weeks.
  • Track Blood Sugar Levels weekly and adjust portion sizes accordingly.
  • Combine Diet with Lifestyle Practices: Yoga, Walking, Stress Management.
  • Avoid White Rice, Refined Sugar, Processed Foods & Junk Food.
